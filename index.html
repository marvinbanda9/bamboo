About Bamboo Innovation Hub

Bamboo Innovation Hub is committed to promoting bamboo as a sustainable and renewable resource across Africa. We focus on environmental conservation, climate resilience, and economic empowerment by equipping communities with skills in bamboo cultivation, processing, and product development. Our work supports green innovation, reduces deforestation, and creates sustainable livelihoods.
