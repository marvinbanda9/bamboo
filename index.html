About Bamboo Innovation Hub

Bamboo Innovation Hub is a youth-led initiative committed to promoting bamboo as a sustainable resource in Africa. We combine creativity with sustainability to produce bamboo crafts, furniture, fashion, interior products, and construction solutions. Our mission is to empower youth and women with practical skills in bamboo farming, product development, and enterprise creation while restoring ecosystems and reducing environmental degradation.
